About risk scores and exposure levels

The risk score (0–100) and exposure level (Low 0–29, Elevated 30–59, High 60–84, Critical 85–100) are automated estimates based on the issues our scan detects. They don't predict whether you'll be sued, and they don't measure legal liability. Automated scans can't detect every accessibility or privacy issue.

No guarantees

Fixing issues reduces risk. It doesn't make any website immune from claims. We make no guarantee that you won't receive a demand letter or lawsuit.

Statistics

Lawsuit statistics on this site come from published third-party sources, cited with the year under each figure: UsableNet (2025 Year-End Digital Accessibility Lawsuit Report) and Seyfarth Shaw (ADA Title III blog, 2025 data). Derived figures show their math in a footnote.
