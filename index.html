FAQ

Straight answers.

Will Compliantfy stop me from being sued?

No one can promise that, and we won't. Fixing the barriers and privacy gaps that show up in lawsuits lowers your risk and shows good faith. It doesn't make you immune.

Is the risk score legal advice?

No. It's an automated estimate based on the issues our scan detects. For legal questions, talk to a licensed attorney. See our disclaimer.

Do you use an accessibility overlay widget?

No. We fix issues in your actual code and content. UsableNet's 2025 report notes that lawsuits kept being filed against companies using accessibility widgets.

How long does a scan take?

Results in minutes. Bigger sites take longer, and you'll see progress as each check runs.

What laws do you check against?

US first: the ADA with WCAG 2.2 AA as the technical benchmark, CCPA/CPRA and other US state privacy laws, and cookie and pixel consent. Turn on the EU toggle to add GDPR and European Accessibility Act checks.

I already got a demand letter. Can you help?

We can audit and fix your site quickly and document what changed. Your attorney handles the legal response. Request a quote.
