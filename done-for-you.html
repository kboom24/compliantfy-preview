How we work

Audit. Fix. Verify. Watch.

  1. Audit

    Automated scan plus manual testing with a keyboard and screen readers on your key flows: home, search, product, cart, checkout, forms.

  2. Fix

    We fix it in your theme or codebase: labels, alt text, contrast, focus, keyboard, consent, pixels, policies.

  3. Verify

    We re-test every fix and give you a dated fix log and an accessibility statement.

  4. Watch

    A monthly retainer re-scans weekly and fixes new issues when your team ships changes.
